## DNFS research foundation

The sampler implementation follows the equations and algorithms of Ou, Zhang
and Li, *Discrete Neural Flow Samplers with Locally Equivariant Transformer*
(2025). See the [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Wk65okms3T) and
[original repository](https://github.com/J-zin/DNFS). As recorded in the project
README, this implementation is derived from the papers' descriptions. This
acknowledgement is not a claim to ownership of the upstream implementation.
Additional method references are retained in individual module docstrings.

## MetaDNS Cu–Au inputs

[data/ce/cuau_fcc_2x2x4.json](data/ce/cuau_fcc_2x2x4.json) and
[data/ce/cuau_fcc_4x4x4.json](data/ce/cuau_fcc_4x4x4.json) are polynomial exports
of the Cu–Au CLEASE energy model distributed with
[MetaDNS](https://github.com/xiaochendu/metadns). They are generated by this
repository's [exporter](experiments/alloy_ce/export_binary_expansion.py), rather
than copies of the upstream ECI file. Their embedded metadata credits Du et al.
(2026), the ECI fit by Damewood et al. (2022), and the input filenames.

The [upstream licence](https://github.com/xiaochendu/metadns/blob/main/LICENSE)
includes an MIT grant, copyright 2025–2026 Xiaochen Du, Juno Nam, Jaemoo Choi,
Wei Guo, Sathya Edamadaka, Junyi Sha, Elton Pan, Yongxin Chen, Molei Tao and
Rafael Gómez-Bombarelli. An unchanged copy is retained in
[LICENSES/MetaDNS-LICENSE.txt](LICENSES/MetaDNS-LICENSE.txt), including upstream's
notices about its own dependencies. Those notices describe files in MetaDNS;
they do not imply those files are bundled here. The upstream repository notice
does not independently establish the original ECI dataset's licensing history.

## icet tutorial and Ag–Pd reference data

[notebooks/icet_agpb_tutorial.ipynb](notebooks/icet_agpb_tutorial.ipynb) adapts
the [icet getting-started tutorial](https://icet.materialsmodeling.org/get_started/construct_cluster_expansion.html)
and extends it with commentary and diagnostics. Despite the historical filename,
the tutorial concerns Ag–Pd. The adapted tutorial source is distributed under
the Mozilla Public License 2.0, following
[icet's upstream licence](https://gitlab.com/materials-modeling/icet/-/blob/master/LICENSE).
The licence text is retained in
[LICENSES/icet-MPL-2.0.txt](LICENSES/icet-MPL-2.0.txt).

[data/reference_data.db](data/reference_data.db) is an unchanged copy of
[icet's tutorial database](https://gitlab.com/materials-modeling/icet/-/blob/master/examples/tutorial/reference_data.db).
The upstream and local files were compared on 7 September 2026 and have SHA-256
`70b58e0cede2bec324dcf2fb62b0243d5630ce7a1aff235fc91206513546e0f1`.
It retains its upstream provenance and terms; no separate dataset licence was
identified in this review, and no new MIT grant is asserted over it.

The notebook's fitted [mixing_energy.ce](notebooks/mixing_energy.ce) and retained
PNG plots are derived tutorial outputs. They remain associated with this
provenance and are excluded from the project's grant over original material.
The independently fitted square Cu–Au toy model in [icet-ce/](icet-ce/README.md)
and its [JSON exports](data/README.md) are a separate workflow.
